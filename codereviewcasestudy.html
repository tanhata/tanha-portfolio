Code Review Intelligence Platform

Designing AI-powered workflow optimization for engineering teams

Role: Sr Product Design Engineer
Duration: 6 months
Team: 3 Developers, 5 Designers, 2 IT, 3 SRE
Users: 50 developers across 3 internal teams and 5 external visiting teams

01. Problem Discovery

Through initial stakeholder interviews, I identified that our engineering team was experiencing significant velocity issues. Code reviews were taking an average of 3.2 days, well above industry benchmarks of 1.8 days.
  • 73% of deployment delays traced to review bottlenecks
  • Uneven workload distribution creating single points of failure
  • No visibility into review queue health or bottleneck formation
  • Manual assignment process based on availability, not expertise

02. User Research

I conducted 12 contextual interviews with engineering managers and 23 surveys with developers across different seniority levels to understand the review workflow pain points.

04. Final Solution

The final interface focuses on Maya's core workflow: rapid health assessment, intelligent prioritization, and predictive bottleneck prevention.
⚡ Live Interactive Demo

Key Design Features

  • Expertise Matching: Analyzes historical code contributions to match reviewers with relevant domain knowledge
  • Workload Balancing: Real-time queue analysis prevents any reviewer from becoming overwhelmed
  • Learning Opportunities: Strategic pairing of junior and senior developers for knowledge transfer
  • Context Preservation: Groups related changes to minimize reviewer context switching
  • Bottleneck Detection: Identifies potential delays 2-3 days before they impact timelines
  • Capacity Forecasting: Helps with sprint planning by predicting review bandwidth
  • Priority Correlation: Flags high-business-impact PRs at risk of delays
  • Team Health Monitoring: Tracks sustainable workload distribution
